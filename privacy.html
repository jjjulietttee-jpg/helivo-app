What we touch, what we never do

Helivo is a pocket studio, so most of this page is about things that stay in your pocket.

Stays on your iPhone

  • Voice recordings (takes)
  • Video you film in the app
  • Voice effects, rendered offline
  • Captions, written by iOS speech recognition set to run on-device
  • Your list of takes and settings

Never sent by Helivo

  • Your voice, to any server
  • Your face or camera video
  • Your contacts or location
  • Your photo library contents
  • Anything for advertising

Why it asks for permissions

Microphone to record you. Camera to film the clip. Speech recognition to write captions; Helivo requires the on-device mode, and if a language is not available that way, captions are simply not made rather than sent away. Photos, add only to drop a finished video into your library when you tap Save. Helivo never reads what is already in Photos.

What does leave the phone

Just the subscription side. Apple handles payment. We use Adapty to find out whether Pro is on and to show the right offer, and Adapty gets an anonymous profile number plus purchase and screen-step events (for example that a paywall appeared). It never gets recordings, video or captions. If you open Write to us, your mail app starts a note carrying the build, your iOS release and that profile number so we can find your purchase; feel free to delete the lines.

Taking it all away

Open a take and tap Delete in the Delete this take? sheet: the video and the voice are removed from Helivo. Copies you saved to Photos remain, so remove those in Photos if you want them gone too. Deleting the app erases everything it holds. There is nothing on our side to erase, because we never received it.
