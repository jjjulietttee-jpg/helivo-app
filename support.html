Quick fixes

I bought Pro and it is locked again.

Tap Restore on the paywall, using the same Apple account that paid. Still stuck? E-mail us the order number from Apple.

There is no sound, or my voice is not recorded.

Open iOS Settings, Helivo, and make sure Microphone is on. Check that the silent switch and your volume are not muting playback, and that no call or other recording app is holding the mic.

The camera is black.

Allow Camera in iOS Settings, Helivo. Close other apps that are using the camera and try again.

Captions are missing.

They need Speech Recognition allowed and a language that iOS can handle on the device. Helivo does not fall back to the internet for this.

How do I delete a take?

Open it and choose delete, then confirm in the sheet. Clips you saved to Photos need to be deleted from Photos separately.

How do I remove the Helivo mark?

It comes off for Pro members: tap Remove the Helivo mark on the take screen.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Helivo, Cancel.
