Ground rules

Be kind with other people's voices, and here is how the paid part works.

Free and Pro

You can use the free voices without paying. Pro covers the paid features and lets you export without the Helivo mark. It comes weekly, opening with three free days if you qualify, or yearly. Your local price and whether a trial applies are on the purchase screen before you confirm.

Money mechanics

You pay Apple, using your Apple ID. A plan keeps going on its own, term after term, until you turn auto-renew off at least a day before the term ends. A trial you do not cancel in time becomes a paid plan. Stop it in iOS Settings under your name, Subscriptions, Helivo. Deleting the app does not stop billing. Refund questions go to Apple.

Voices and consent

Changing a voice is fun, and it can also be used to mislead. Do not use Helivo to impersonate a real person, to deceive or harass anyone, to record people without their knowledge where that is unlawful, or to make content that breaks the law or the rules of the platform you post on. You are responsible for what you record and share.

Your clips

Your recordings and videos belong to you. Helivo only needs your permission to process them on your phone to build the result. Captions come from on-device speech recognition and can contain mistakes, so read them before posting.

If something goes wrong

The app comes as it is. We try to keep it working, but we cannot promise it will never crash, lose a take or fit every device. Keep important recordings backed up. To the extent the law lets us, we are not liable for indirect losses. Consumer rights you have by law are untouched, and these terms follow the law of the place where the publisher is based.

Apple's terms, and edits
